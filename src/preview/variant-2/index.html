Editorial statement

Why the doors are open.

A living tradition is a plural one. We publish the sources, the disagreements, and the working notes so any reader may weigh them for themselves.

There is no single Celtic orthodoxy — there never was. What we offer is a museum's honesty: an accession record for every claim, a citation for every source, and gallery labels that admit what remains uncertain.
