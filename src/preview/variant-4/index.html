Why it is open

Kept in common.

A living tradition is a plural one. We publish the sources, the disagreements, and the working notes so any reader may weigh them for themselves.

There is no single Celtic orthodoxy — there never was. What we offer is an honestly-attributed reference: what a text says, when it was written, who copied it, and how much of the practice around it has been reconstructed. Corrections are welcome; provenance is required.
